Public Offer

The terms of study: payments, missed lessons and refunds.

for the provision of English language training services

by Sevenine Center (LLC “7-NINE”)

1. General Provisions

1.1. This public offer (hereinafter referred to as the “Offer”) governs the relationship between Sevenine Center (LLC “7-NINE”), hereinafter referred to as the “Center”, and an individual, hereinafter referred to as the “Student.”

1.2. The Center provides English language training services in offline and/or online formats, depending on the selected tariff.

1.3. Acceptance (acceptance of the Offer) is deemed to occur upon any of the following actions:

– payment for the course;

– participation in a lesson;

– use of the Center’s bonus features (application, Speaking Workshops, etc.).

1.4. Acceptance of this Offer signifies full and unconditional agreement with its terms.

2. Terms and Definitions

For the purposes of this Offer, the following terms are used as defined below:

  • Center — the educational institution Sevenine (LLC “7-NINE”) providing English language training services.
  • Student — an individual who has accepted the terms of this Offer and uses the services of the Center.
  • Lesson — an educational session conducted by a teacher at the time specified in the schedule.
  • Tariff — the cost and format of the course established by the Center.

3. Obligations of the Parties

3.1. The Center undertakes to:

  • conduct lessons in accordance with the approved schedule;
  • provide a substitute teacher in case of force majeure circumstances (illness, personal reasons, etc.);
  • reschedule lessons if necessary, with prior notice and agreement with students;
  • ensure quality control of the educational process through video surveillance systems with audio and video recording, used exclusively for internal evaluation and security purposes.

3.2. The Student undertakes to:

  • attend lessons in accordance with the established schedule;
  • complete homework assignments conscientiously and on time;
  • maintain discipline and treat teachers and other students with respect;
  • take proper care of the Center’s property;
  • make payment for tuition in advance, before the start of each new study period (4 weeks).

3.3. If a Student misses more than two lessons per month without a valid reason and/or systematically fails to complete homework assignments, the Center reserves the right to expel the Student without refund of the paid fees.

4. Rights of the Center

4.1. The Center has the right to send informational and promotional SMS messages to students.

4.2. The Center may change the schedule and bonus programs, provided that students are notified in advance.

4.3. The Center has the right to amend the terms of this Offer without prior notice.

5. Payment and Refund Policy

5.1. Payment is made in advance for each 4-week study period.

5.2. A trial (demo) lesson is provided free of charge if the student decides not to continue the course. If the student continues, the demo lesson is considered the first paid lesson.

5.3. Lessons missed due to the Student’s fault are not compensated and are not rescheduled.

5.4. A refund is possible only if the Center is unable to continue providing the lessons. In such cases, the refund amount corresponds to the unconducted lessons, while fees for lessons already conducted are non-refundable.

6. Cost of Services

6.1. The cost of tuition depends on the selected tariff and the form of study.

6.2. Current prices are published on Sevenine’s official information resources or provided by an administrator upon request.

7. Bonus Features

7.1. The Center may provide students with additional bonuses, including participation in Speaking Workshops, use of the Sevenine application, access to the Sevenine Shop, and other features.

7.2. These bonuses are not a mandatory part of the agreement and may be modified or discontinued without prior notice.
