If you once decide to leave …

A letter from the founder: what to report and how.

All suggestions and complaints can be sent to the company owner on Telegram: t.me/islom13.

✉️

Dear Parents and Students,

My name is Islom.

It is important to me that you feel comfortable studying at Sevenine and that the lessons bring real progress that you can feel and clearly see.

We carefully select our teachers and administrators and train them to work according to our standards. However, sometimes situations may arise that I do not notice immediately.

If you notice anything that interferes with your learning or causes discomfort, please write to me. I am always ready to look into the issue and improve the situation.

Below, I have listed the points that are especially important to report.

Thank you for helping us maintain high quality at Sevenine.

— Islom, Director and Founder of Sevenine

🔥1. Serious Teaching Quality Issues

Factors that directly slow down your progress:

  • lack of preparation; the teacher is “teaching on the fly”
  • low enthusiasm, weak energy, sleepy or disengaged state
  • frequent distractions by phone or external factors
  • insufficient useful material; feeling of no progress
  • unclear explanations of topics
  • going off-topic; low lesson intensity
  • weak student engagement
  • the lesson does not match your level or goals
  • the teacher consistently ends lessons noticeably early or starts late

⚠️2. Professional Conduct and Ethics Violations

Serious cases that require immediate reporting:

  • aggression, irritability, rudeness
  • toxic comments or humiliating behavior
  • use of inappropriate or unprofessional language
  • strong unpleasant odors (sweat, cigarettes, etc.)
  • any form of physical contact
  • discussion of personal problems that violates professional boundaries

🔍3. Classroom Environment and Conditions

Issues that affect comfort:

  • unpleasant smells or stuffy air
  • dusty surfaces, dirt, or an unclean classroom
  • poor discipline in the group due to the teacher’s inaction

😊4. Administrator’s Work During the First Visit

Please inform us if the administrator:

  • did not stand up to greet you during the first meeting
  • did not ask your name
  • did not introduce themselves
  • did not ask about your goal for learning English
  • did not provide a full course presentation
  • was cold, dry, or unsmiling
  • left an unpleasant impression

❤️5. If You Don’t Feel Progress or Are Thinking About Leaving

Before making a decision, please write to me.

I will personally review your situation, help improve the learning process, and offer additional support options.

Your feedback helps me maintain a high standard of education at Sevenine.

Thank you for your honesty and trust.

You can send all suggestions and complaints directly to the company director on Telegram:

t.me/islom13. If you do not use Telegram, you can call me at +998 90 919 79 62.
