Sevenine Shop

How to earn coins and spend them in the Sevenine Shop.

Sevenine Shop is a reward system designed to encourage top-performing students at Sevenine. Students who demonstrate strong academic performance can earn coins, which may be exchanged for items in the Sevenine Shop within the mobile app or on the website.

The shop’s assortment is updated regularly.

How to earn coins

1–5Coins
In class

Good Performance

awarded by tutors

For flawless completion of homework, understanding homework topics very well and active participation in the lesson.

Important: good performance is not equal to satisfying performance. Also, if a student is late to the lesson, even by 1 minute, he/she won’t be able to earn any coins.

Coins for a review on maps

Leave a review about Sevenine on a map and get 5 coins for each platform — up to 20 coins for all four. Scan the QR codes on the poster at school or use the buttons below.

Yandex Maps+5Google Maps+52GIS+5Apple Maps+5
  1. Open a platform — scan its QR code on the poster or tap a button above.
  2. Write 2–3 sentences in your own words about your real experience.
  3. Show your review to the administrator — the coins arrive the same day.

Write it yourself — generic reviews get deleted

Maps automatically remove reviews that look copied or made from a template. A deleted review earns no coins.

✗ These get deleted
  • “Great school, I recommend it!”
  • The same text on every platform
  • Text copied from the internet or from a friend
✓ Answer 2–3 of these in your own words
  • What level do you study, and what do you like about your lessons?
  • What is your teacher or your small group (up to 8 people) like?
  • What can you do in English now that you couldn’t before?
  • How do you feel about the atmosphere or the schedule?

Write a different text for each platform. Be honest — we care about your real experience, and the coins are for the review itself, not for the stars.

When you get no coins

No Coins can be awarded during the whole class

  • A student comes late to the lesson (even if 1 minute late)A student won’t be able to earn any coin during the lesson if he turns up late.
  • A student is a debtorOnce a student’s balance turns negative and has a red dot, this student must not be awarded any coin at all.

Good to know

  • The total amount of coins to be awarded per group is 20. So, if one student gets 20 coins, others won’t be able to earn any.

  • A student with a negative balance cannot earn any coins. So if there is a red dot before a student’s name in the CRM system, a tutor cannot award any coins.

  • A student with a negative balance cannot redeem his/her coins in the Sevenine shop.

  • A student who comes late to the lesson cannot earn any coins.

  • Coins can be deducted at a tutor’s discretion if a student regularly comes late, misses many lessons, or does not do homework.

Note: Sevenine reserves the right, at its sole discretion and at any time without prior notice, to modify, suspend, or discontinue the Sevenine Shop feature, including the earning and use of coins, as well as the availability of any goods or services in the Sevenine Shop, without any obligation.
