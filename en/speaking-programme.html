Speaking Programme

Practise speaking at home: collocations, idioms and questions for every unit.

The Sevenine Speaking Programme is our online platform for speaking practice. Use it at home to get ready for speaking in class and in your tests.

Open the Speaking Programmesevenineuz.github.io/speakingprogramme

What’s inside

  • Every level: Beginner, Elementary, Pre-Intermediate, Intermediate and Upper-Intermediate, plus IELTS Speaking.
  • Collocations and idioms for every unit — the phrases native speakers really use — and speaking questions to practise them.
  • Useful phrases with Russian and Uzbek translations.
  • Test yourself: answer the questions against a timer, like in a real speaking test.

How to practise

  1. Open the platform. Make sure Student is selected at the top right.
  2. Pick your level and the unit you are studying now.
  3. Learn the unit’s collocations and idioms, then answer the speaking questions out loud, in full sentences.
  4. Try to use the useful phrases in your answers.
  5. Before your mid-term and final tests, check yourself with Test yourself. The platform shows how many units you’ve studied.

A little every day works better than a lot once a week: 10 minutes of speaking practice a day makes a real difference.
