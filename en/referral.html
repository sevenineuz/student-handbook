Referral Discounts

Bring a friend and get 250,000 UZS off or 150 coins.

For each friend you refer who pays the full course fee, you can receive a one-time discount of 250,000 UZS or 150 coins in the Sevenine Shop. Discounts are credited as follows:

250,000 UZSoff your course
or
150 coinsin the Sevenine Shop

Referral discounts don’t apply to kids courses. Kids have a family discount and coins for bringing a friend — see below.

How the discount works when you have a positive balance

📅When does the discount start?

If you already have a positive balance, the discount does not apply immediately. It starts from the beginning of the next paid period, after your current balance has been fully used.

📌Example:

  • Course fee: 1,090,000 UZS for 4 weeks
  • Your balance: 545,000 UZS (enough for 2 weeks)
  • Today’s date: September 1

➡️This means:

  • You first continue studying using your current balance
  • The discount starts on September 16, at the beginning of the new study period

💰Discount amount and duration

  • Total discount amount: 250,000 UZS
  • Validity period: 8 weeks (2 months)
  • The discount is divided into two periods:
    • Month 1: −125,000 UZS
    • Month 2: −125,000 UZS

📌Payment example with the discount:

  • Instead of 1,090,000 UZS, you pay 965,000 UZS
  • This applies for two consecutive months
  • ✅Total savings — 250,000 UZS

⚠️If your friend leaves after the first month

  • If the friend you referred stops studying after the first month, the rest of the discount is cancelled (for example, the −125,000 UZS for month 2).

How the discount works if you have an outstanding balance

⚠️Main rule

The discount applies only after the outstanding balance is fully paid.

If there is any debt, the discount does not apply.

💳Payment calculation example

  • Your outstanding balance: 250,000 UZS
  • Course price with discount: 965,000 UZS

Total amount to pay:

  • 250,000 UZS — debt repayment
  • 965,000 UZS — payment for the new course with the discount

✅Total: 1,215,000 UZS

📅When does the discount start?

The discount is activated from the date of the next lesson, not from the payment date.

📌Example:

  • You make the payment on September 10
  • The next lesson is on September 12

➡️The discount starts on September 12.

🚫Important to know

  • ❌The discount does not apply if you have an outstanding balance.
  • ❌The discount cannot be applied retroactively.
  • ✅First — debt repayment, then — the discounted course.

Kids courses: family discount and coins for a friend

Referral discounts don’t apply to kids courses. Instead, kids have a family discount and coins for bringing a friend (2026 prices).

Two or more kids in the kids course

800,000 UZSper 4 weeks for each child

If two or more children from one family study in the kids course, each child studies at the discounted price of 800,000 UZS per 4 weeks.

One child in the kids course, another in General English

The child in the General English course can get the referral discount as usual, or choose 150 coins instead.

or
150 coins75 to each child, or 150 to one child

A child brings a friend to a kids group

If a child from a kids group brings a friend to a kids group, there’s no referral discount, but once the friend pays for the course, the child gets 150 coins.
