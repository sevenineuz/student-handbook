Weekly Reports

For parents: what the weekly report shows and how to help at home.

Every week, parents receive a short report on how their child is doing. It’s a bonus from Sevenine: it keeps you up to date, and it helps us work together — children make the most progress when teachers and parents support them as a team.

How you receive it

  • At the end of each week, the teacher fills in the report.
  • The Sevenine administrator sends it to you on Telegram as a PDF file.

How to read the report

  1. Header — your child’s name, group, teacher and the week the report is for.
  2. Overall grade — the result of the week, from 1 to 3. 3 — excellent: your child uses everything covered confidently. 2 — average: the basics are understood, but they aren’t automatic yet. 1 — poor: the material hasn’t been learned yet.
  3. Star of the Week — a special award for outstanding effort and progress. Only a few students get it, and the teacher explains why.
  4. Skills — speaking, listening, reading, writing, grammar and vocabulary, each from 1 to 5 stars. “Not assessed” means the skill wasn’t the focus that week.
  5. Teacher’s observation — the most important part: what stood out this week.
  6. Tip for home — one concrete thing to do at home this week.

How you can help

  • Read the report together with your child and praise what went well.
  • Do the “Tip for home” together during the week.
  • Check that homework is done and new words are learned — a few every day, not all in one evening.
  • Make sure your child comes on time: being late even by 1 minute means no coins for that lesson.
  • Have a question about the report? Write to @ask_sevenine on Telegram.
